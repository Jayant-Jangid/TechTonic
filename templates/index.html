2. Best Months to Sell (Selling Calendar)

Know when to hold and when to sell for highest profit. High bars mean higher prices in market.

⚠️ Low Price (Harvest Rush) 💰 Highest Price (Off-Season)
When NOT to Sell in a Panic: Nov & May
Market Flood

During harvest months (November for Kharif and May for Rabi), lakhs of farmers bring produce to mandis at the same time. Traders drop prices by 30% to 50%.

💡 Simple Advice: If you have dry space or village godown, hold your grain or onion for 4 to 6 weeks to avoid selling at a loss.
Best Months for Top Price: Aug & Sep
+50% Higher Profit

By August and September, mandi arrivals drop heavily and market demand is peak. Stored crops like onions, pulses, and grains sell for top dollar.

💰 Simple Advice: Release your stored produce during August–September. Buyers on KisanSetu pay maximum rates during this window.
